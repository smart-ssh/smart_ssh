Why B “Trace”

An S drawn as one orthogonal connection trace between two terminals: client and server. It is a monogram for Smart SSH, it does not depend on the generic >_ prompt every terminal app uses, and it reads at 16 px because it has only three strokes and two solid squares. The accent squares are the only colour, so the mark survives one-colour use (print, tray icon, monochrome macOS menu bar template).

Grid: 8-unit modules, 8-unit clear space (dashed). Stroke 7 u in tiles, 9 u for the bare 16 px favicon. Minimum size: 16 px glyph, 24 px tile. A keeps continuity with today's >_ icon and adds the filter gate; C puts the AI (diamond) inside the guard brackets.
